# Quark & Cosmos

An Android physics puzzle game (working title): Quarky, a particle born in a lab experiment, travels from the quantum scale to the cosmological one, and each law of physics becomes a touch puzzle. The app is in English and French.

- **Play the POC**: open `android/` in Android Studio ([android/README.md](android/README.md)).
- **Build levels**: `levels-builder/` ([README](levels-builder/README.md)); the shipped pack is in `content/`.
- **Design**: `design/` (art direction board, mockups).
- **Specs & decisions**: [`docs/physics-spec.md`](docs/physics-spec.md), [`docs/level-schema.md`](docs/level-schema.md), [`docs/decisions/`](docs/decisions/README.md).
- **Roadmap**: [`todo.md`](todo.md). **Working rules, stage status, commands**: [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md).
