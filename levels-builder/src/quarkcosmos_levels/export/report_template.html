Star progression — Quantum world

For each level and each difficulty, a dense fan of launches (angle, power, tap instant) is simulated. Among valid launches (target reached), the share earning at least k stars should follow a truncated gaussian exp(−k² / 2σ²), with σ shrinking as difficulty grows.
