# design/worlds — the four future worlds (`[FULL]` scope, design exploration)

Style frames for the four scales after Quantum, built on the validated art direction v2 (skill `art-direction`: flat B matter + cinematic C background, Quarky v2, HUD = instrument eyepiece, portrait). Open `index.html` in a browser (self-contained; only the Google Fonts JetBrains Mono and Fira Sans are loaded). Quantum is shown in every section as the validated reference column.

**Status: exploration only.** Nothing here changes the beta scope (Quantum, 7 levels) or a validated decision. Every proposal below needs a user decision before it becomes a spec (see "Open questions").

Page sections: 01 overview · 02 colour contracts + live WCAG table · 03 instruments and C backgrounds (with a background/matter luminance probe) · 04 Quarky mutations (idle, flight, 32/48/96 px) · 05 level screens 390 × 844 with EN/FR panel toggle, success/failure outcome, label-width table · 06 scales screen + one world map per world · 07 force registry and colour-blind checks · 08 physics check.

Trajectories are computed in the page, then the portal and Photons are placed on them (Coulomb for Atomic, spring + ramp + free fall for Macro, Newton with a moving planet for Space, Newtonian attraction for Quarky and a thin-lens picture for light in Cosmological). The mockup physics is not the reference: `docs/physics-spec.md` is, and the level generator decides real geometry.

## The worlds

**Atomic & molecular (`#facc15`, 10⁻⁹ m).** Instrument: a scanning tunnelling microscope — the eyepiece shows the STM tip riding the top of the frame; the C background is an out-of-focus atomic lattice (hexagonal bokeh), an electron-density fog and dust, no grid. Contract: key `#facc15`, accent `#60a5fa` (blue = the charge satellite and the − ions, the yellow/blue axis survives deuteranopia and protanopia), background `#0c0a05`, threshold `#fb7185`, derived `#86efac` (so the Photon energies run yellow → green → blue, in spectral order), HUD `#fdf8e8` / `#c8bd9a`. Quarky's mutation: an orbiting electron ring and a blue satellite bearing a − glyph (the charge). Level mock "Coulomb's law": radial topology around a + nucleus; Quarky (−) orbits it, a + ion attracts, a − ion repels, each ion has a sign chip (±) and a notched radial rail (distance). Physics layer: yellow chevron field lines from + to −, dash-dot equipotentials drawn through each Photon ("Photons sit on equipotential lines"), rose long-dash capture ring. Readings: Quarky's charge, ion distance, Coulomb force ∝ 1/r² with an ATTRACTS tag. Map: a spiral going outwards from the nucleus.

**Macro (`#4ade80`, 1 m).** Instrument: the high-speed bench camera (chronophotography) with a metric ruler in the frame; the C background is the lab out of focus — lamp bokeh, the scientist at a far bench (first appearance, 7 % alpha, low frequency), dust — plus the very discreet technical grid the skill reserves for Macro. Contract: key `#4ade80`, accent `#f0abfc` (magenta turns blue in deuteranopia, so it stays apart from the green), background `#050d09`, threshold `#fb923c`, derived `#fde68a`, HUD `#effbf3` / `#a7c4b1`. Quarky: canonical form, no extra part. Level mock "Energy conservation": a horizontal corridor with lateral tracking (mini-map); the launcher is a spring (k = 400 N/m, x = 0.35 m → 24.5 J), a 60° ramp, a free-flight arc whose top Photon (2.02 m) is only reachable with exactly this energy, and a portal on a platform. The readings show a live energy bar where Ec (solid), Ep gravity (hatched) and Ep spring (zigzag) swap while the total stays constant; at the top Ec = 4.7 J, not zero. Trail variant: chronophotography outlines every 0.1 s. Map: switchback shelves of the lab.

**Space (`#a78bfa`, 10⁹ m).** Instrument: a tracking telescope (reticle ticks); the C background is sparse soft stars (bokeh, never pinpoints behind the play area), a faint nebula and dust. Contract: key `#a78bfa`, accent `#fcd34d` (the star), background `#07050f`, threshold `#f87171`, derived `#f9a8d4`, HUD `#f3effd` / `#b4a9d0`. Quarky's mutation: mini solar-sail fins and a star trail. Level mock "Gravity assist": an open plane with two wells (giant ringed planet + moon); the planet moves (its own dotted path and a V arrow) and Quarky passes behind it to leave faster in the star's frame. Physics layer: blue dash-chevron gravity lines, dash-dot well contours, red long-dash minimum distance; the aim dots are one per equal time step, so they spread out at closest approach (Kepler's second law). Readings: speed in the star frame (dashed mark = launch speed), distance to the planet, gravity ∝ 1/r². Map: one orbit around the star.

**Cosmological (`#818cf8`, 10²² m) — the climax.** Instrument: a radio interferometer + gravitational-wave observatory that "sees space-time"; the C background is low-frequency CMB-like mottling and the curved grid, which is the level itself. Contract: key `#818cf8`, accent `#fde68a` (the Einstein ring), background `#04040c`, threshold `#fb923c`, derived `#f0abfc`, HUD `#eef0ff` / `#a9aed6`. Quarky's mutation: a skewed silhouette stretched towards the mass (stronger closer to the hole). Level mock "Gravitational lensing": the straight shot would cross the photon sphere; the player aims beside the opaque black hole and space-time bends the path to the portal. Light rays from a background galaxy bend around the hole and meet at the eyepiece (Einstein ring); Photons sit on the ring, and the third one needs a tighter route. The setting reacts: the grid pinches around the hole, dimples under Quarky, sends a gravitational-wave ripple on success and shudders red on failure; the eyepiece frame itself is slightly bowed. Readings: passing distance b, deflection α ∝ M/b (live), black-hole mass. Map: a spiral inwards, the last node on the black hole.

## Open design questions (would need a `[GATE]` decision)

1. **Colour contracts** of the four worlds (tables above), in particular: Atomic threshold rose `#fb7185` instead of the Quantum orange (too close to the yellow key); Macro keeps orange `#fb923c` although it is weak against the green under deuteranopia (doubled by hatching + long dashes) — accept, or pick a blue/violet danger?
2. **Quarky's colour across scales**: the mocks give Quarky each scale's key colour (consistent with "one colour grade per scale"). Alternative: keep a constant pink core as an identity anchor across the journey.
3. **Force registry game-wide** (section 07): new forces and their colours/styles — electric `#fde047` chevrons, equipotential `#94a3b8` dash-dot, Van der Waals `#5eead4` comb, gravity `#93c5fd` dash + hollow chevrons (one gravity colour for Macro, Space and Cosmological), spring `#86efac` zigzag, light `#fef3c7` wavefront ticks, Hubble flow `#fca5a5` growing dashes. The threshold keeps one style (long dashes) but takes each scale's threshold colour: OK, or should "danger" be one game-wide colour?
4. **Aim preview dots spaced by equal time steps** (not equal distances) in every world: in Quantum (no gravity, constant speed) nothing changes, elsewhere the spacing shows speed (Kepler). Adopt as a game-wide rule?
5. **Instruments and their names**: STM / bench camera / tracking telescope / interferometer array. The shipped strings `world_*_instrument` read "Electron cloud / The lab / Beyond the Earth / Curved space-time": keep them as poetic subtitles or align them with the instrument?
6. **Macro chronophotography trail** (outlines every 0.1 s) instead of the simple B line: a Macro-only variant or not at all?
7. **The scientist's first appearance** as a blurred background silhouette in Macro, before any dialogue: is the background the right place, and how far can it go without breaking the ≤ 20 % luminance rule?
8. **Macro camera**: the corridor with lateral tracking inside a portrait screen (mini-map at the top of the frame). Alternative: stack the corridor into vertical switchbacks so no tracking is needed.
9. **Gravity assist with a moving planet**: the speed gain is only real if the planet moves in the level (in the planet's frame speed in = speed out). The engine must then simulate moving wells; a static planet can only teach deflection.
10. **Cosmological lensing model**: the mock bends Quarky with Newtonian attraction and light with a thin-lens rule (α = K/b). Decide the game rule for the engine, and the "In real physics…" note (light is bent twice as much as the Newtonian estimate; Quarky and light bend differently).
11. **Cosmological reactions**: the grid dimple under Quarky, the success ripple (gravitational wave) and the red shudder on failure — enough for "the setting reacts to gameplay", and within the mobile budget (grid redrawn every frame)?
12. **Unlock thresholds** shown on the scales screen (★ 14, 28, 42, 56 ≈ 2 stars per level of the previous world): placeholder until closed-test data (gameplay-mechanics).
13. **World map shapes** per topology (spiral out, switchback shelves, one orbit, spiral in): adopt, or keep the Quantum winding path everywhere for consistency?
14. **Concept lists per world** used as map nodes (7 each, with "II" repeats and a Mix node) are placeholders taken from `todo.md` §2.2; the real list and order is a `gameplay-mechanics` decision.
