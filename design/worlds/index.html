01

The five scales at a glance

One instrument per scale; the HUD is its eyepiece. Composition goes from fixed and tight (Quantum, Atomic) to lateral tracking (Macro), free zoom (Space) and zoom + distortion (Cosmological), as set by the art-direction skill.

02

Colour contracts

Same format as Quantum: key + complementary accent + deep background + threshold/danger + derived colour + HUD text pair. Photon energies are always key (4 rays) → derived (6 rays) → accent (8 rays). WCAG ratios are computed live below (and listed in the README); the worst case is the text on the solid eyepiece bezel.

03

Instruments and C-style backgrounds

Each background is three parallax layers (distant bokeh → fringes/fog → foreground dust) with grain and vignette at 3 %, no high-frequency detail behind the play area. The probe measures the background's mean luminance against the matter layer of the matching level screen (rule: ≤ 20 %).

04

Quarky's mutations

Same DNA everywhere: jelly membrane, glowing core, big dark eyes with a white highlight, gummy highlight, ~1.5 px outline. The Macro form is canonical; the other four are mutations of it. Real-size tests at 48 px and 32 px (CSS pixels, no scaling).

05

Level screens — portrait 390 × 844

Same skeleton as the Android LevelScreen: top bar (back, 3 Photon counters, title, reset), the instrument's play area, and the bottom “instrument readings” panel. Demo loop: aim → flight → Photons → portal, and the setting's reaction to the outcome.

06

Scales screen and world maps

The scales list keeps the Compose layout (tile = emblem + magnitude + name + instrument, dotted journey thread between tiles); the emblem becomes the world's Quarky mutation. Each world map keeps the Quantum map's vocabulary (portal-ring nodes, dotted path solid up to the current node, Quarky on the current node) but its path follows the world's topology.

07
