# AgSoft developer splash: two options

The studio splash is shown on cold start, before the Quark & Cosmos welcome screen. Both options use the same silver studio identity: flat "B-style" matter (flat fills, shadow crescent, facet, highlight dot, 1.5 px outline) on the game's near-black `#0a0612`, with one soft bloom gradient. Both end by fading to `#0a0612` so the hand-over to the welcome screen is seamless. Each page autoplays, has Replay, 0.25× speed and EN/FR toggles, respects `prefers-reduced-motion` (still logo), and shows icon-size previews plus contrast checks. The facts are the same in both: silver is Ag, 47, 107.87, from Latin *argentum*, a soft and malleable metal; quicksilver is mercury, Hg, 80, 200.59, liquid at room temperature, from *hydrargyrum*, "water-silver".

**Option A: Struck silver** ([option-a.html](option-a.html), about 2.45 s). A clean periodic-table tile, "47 · Ag · Silver · 107.87". Its outline is traced while the atomic number counts up to 47, then a metallic sheen crosses it (with a hair-thin pink/cyan fringe that nods to the Quantum palette). The tile is struck like a malleable metal: it squashes, springs back, its corners round off and the "Ag" glyphs swell and soften. It then slides left and "Soft" slides out from behind it, so the final lock-up reads **[Ag]Soft** on one line, with the tagline "argentum · soft silver" (FR "argentum · métal malléable"). On Android, the tile is the Android 12 SplashScreen animated icon (an AVD of about 600 ms: outline trim-path and fill). A Compose `Canvas` sequence then picks it up at the same position (spring squash, glyph path with an animated round-join stroke, clip reveal). There is no blur, bitmap or video.

**Option B: Quicksilver drop** ([option-b.html](option-b.html), about 2.5 s). A liquid quicksilver drop falls and stretches with its speed, then splashes. Thin dotted ripples spread (invisible-physics line style) and droplets fall back into the liquid. The liquid gathers and rises into a jelly-wobbling square that sets into a crisp tile. For a beat the tile reads "80 · Hg · Mercury · 200.59", then it recalibrates to "47 · Ag · Silver · 107.87" (an alchemist's wink, stated as such, not chemistry). One quicksilver bead stays on the corner, and it is also the icon's signature. The vertical lock-up puts the tile above an **AgSoft** wordmark that fills from below behind a wavy liquid level. The tagline is "quicksilver at heart" (FR "du vif-argent dans les veines"). On Android, the system splash is background-only, and one Compose `Canvas` draws everything. The liquid is keyframed path morphs, not the SVG goo filter used in the mockup; the droplets are circles on parabolas and the ripples are dashed ovals. There is no blur, bitmap or video.
